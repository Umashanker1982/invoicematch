# Phase 2: Scoping and success metrics

Turns the discovery findings into a bounded first release and a way to prove it worked.

| Document | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [scope.md](scope.md) | What release 1 does and does not do; the decision rules agreed with Compliance |
| [success_metrics.md](success_metrics.md) | The five SMART metrics, with baseline, targets, measurement, and owner |

**In one line:** release 1 automates routine, PO-backed digital invoices up to $10,000, explains every exception, and keeps people in charge of everything else. Target: 50% no-touch invoices and 57% less manual effort by month 6, paying back in about 7 months.
