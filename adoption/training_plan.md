# Training plan

A technically correct system that nobody uses is a failed engagement. Training is built around what each group was worried about in discovery, and scheduled away from month-end (the last 5 working days).

## Structure: Prosci ADKAR

Each person needs Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, and Reinforcement. The plan covers all five, not only the "how to click" part.

| Stage | What it means here | How we do it |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** (why change?) | The team works at full capacity; 15% more invoices are coming; money leaks through duplicates | CFO kick-off message with the business case; AP Manager team meeting |
| **Desire** (what's in it for me?) | Less copy-typing, calmer month-ends, **no layoffs** | CFO's written no-layoff commitment; specialists help design the review screen |
| **Knowledge** (how?) | Reading the decision, the ten checks, approving and rejecting | Role-based sessions below; one-page guides; the demo site as a practice sandbox |
| **Ability** (can do it on real work) | Handling real exceptions confidently | Pilot users work next to an FDE engineer in week one; practice set of 20 tricky invoices |
| **Reinforcement** (keeps it going) | Habits stick; feedback is acted on | Weekly feedback session; "you said, we did" log; recognition for good catches |

## Sessions by audience

| Audience | Format | Length | Content | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AP Specialists** (6) | Hands-on workshop, 3 people per session | 2 × 90 min | Reading a decision; the ten checks; side-by-side evidence; approve/reject with a reason; what to do when the AI could not read an invoice; how to flag a wrong decision | Each specialist resolves the 20-invoice practice set correctly |
| **AP Manager** | 1:1 walkthrough | 60 min | All of the above, plus the queue dashboard, reassigning work, approval limits, the weekly metrics | Runs the queue alone for a day |
| **Finance Controller, CFO** (approvers) | Short briefing | 20 min | What reaches them and why; high-value mismatches; approving from the queue | Approves one pilot invoice |
| **Compliance** | Working session | 60 min | Rules M-1..M-10 and A-1..A-4; audit log; tamper check; how limits change | Signs off the rules and audit format |
| **Internal Audit** | Working session | 60 min | Weekly 50-decision sample; recording disagreements; reading audit records | Completes the first sample |
| **Procurement buyers** | Team meeting | 30 min | What mismatch messages look like; how they are routed; the weekly report | Understands how to respond to a routed mismatch |
| **Information Security** | Briefing | 30 min | Bank-detail holds (M-9) and the phone-verification procedure; data flow | Clears one test hold |

## Materials

- **One-page quick guide per role** (what you see, what you do, who to ask).
- **Practice sandbox:** the demo website with sample invoices, so people can try things without touching real data.
- **"Reading a decision" cheat sheet:** each rule id in plain words, with the most common fix.
- **Short screen recordings** (3–5 minutes each) for refreshers and new joiners.
