Read the invoice. Check it against the PO. Decide who needs to look.

Pick a sample invoice (or paste your own text) and press Check invoice. Claude reads it through the course's SharedLLM gateway, then written rules compare it with the purchase order and contract and decide: approve automatically, ask the right approver, or send it to a person. AI reads; rules decide; people approve.

How it works

A Forward Deployed Engineering engagement for a fictional finance company whose Accounts Payable team checks supplier invoices against purchase orders by hand. The repository documents the whole engagement: discovery, scoping, design, deployment, adoption, and productization.

Invoicetext of a digital PDF or e-invoice
Document parserreads bank details with fixed patterns and removes them before any AI call
Extraction agentClaude (Haiku) via the SharedLLM gateway returns typed fields
Groundinginvoice no., PO, and totals must appear in the text, or they are discarded
Matching engine10 written checks against PO, contract, and history
Approval routinglimits from the Delegation of Authority
Review queue + audit logrole-based approvals; tamper-evident log; ERP status update

The rules (code, not AI)

Safety built in

  • The AI only reads. It has no tools and cannot approve or pay anything.
  • Bank account numbers never reach the AI model.
  • Invoice text is treated as untrusted data; hidden instructions are ignored.
  • Values the AI returns must be printed on the invoice, and the lines must add up to the printed total.
  • Only matched invoices up to $10,000 are approved automatically.
  • Changed bank details always go to Information Security.
  • If the AI gateway fails, a rules reader takes over; unreadable invoices go to a person, never to payment.
