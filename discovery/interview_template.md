# Stakeholder interview template

Used for every discovery interview so the answers can be compared. Each interview takes 30–45 minutes. Answers are synthesized in [interview_notes.md](interview_notes.md).

**Before the interview**
- Explain the purpose: understand today's invoice process, not to evaluate anyone's work.
- Ask for permission to take notes. Quotes are anonymized in shared material.

**Context**
- Interviewee role:
- Team and size:
- Date and interviewer:

## Questions

1. What is your role in the current process?
2. Where does the process slow down or break?
3. What data do you need to trust the output?
4. What would make you comfortable delegating this task to a system?
5. What happens if the system makes a mistake?
6. Who else should we talk to?
7. What does success look like in three months?

**Follow-up prompts (use when needed)**
- "Can you show me the last time that happened?"
- "How often does that happen in a typical week?"
- "What do you do today as a workaround?"

**After the interview**
- Write up the answers the same day.
- Tag each pain point with a theme (effort, errors, control, trust, people).
- Note any new stakeholders to interview (question 6).
